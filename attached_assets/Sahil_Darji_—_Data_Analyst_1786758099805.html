About

I don't build dashboards.
I build the question they answer.

I'm a final-year B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Data Science student (KJSIT, Mumbai) focused on data analytics and business intelligence. My work spans SQL, Power BI, Excel, Python, and DAX — and every project I ship starts from a business question, not a chart type.

During my finance internship at Yodaplus Technologies, I built AI-powered financial analysis workflows using CrewAI across six industries — that's where I learned that a dashboard nobody trusts is worse than no dashboard at all.

I'm looking for Data Analyst or BI roles where I can sit close to the decision, not just the dataset.

How I actually work

I don't start in Power BI. I start by writing down the decision someone needs to make and what would change their mind. Then I check whether the data can actually answer that — half the time the first version of a "dashboard" I build is just a scratch query to check if the insight is real before I model it. If a metric can't survive "so what would we do differently," I cut it.
